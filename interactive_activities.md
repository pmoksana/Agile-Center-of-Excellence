 ## 4 interactive activities to run with my teams:

1. **The SAFe "Backlog Refinement & WSJF" Poker**

**Objective**: Test if the team understands prioritization, economic view (SAFe Principle #1), and breaking down user stories.

**How to Run**:

Provide the team with 5–6 messy, high-level "Epic" or "Feature" descriptions related to your upcoming work.

Have them work in small groups to apply Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) (Cost of Delay divided by Job Size) to rank them.

Challenge them to take the top-ranked feature and slice it down into small, vertical user stories that can fit into a single 2-week iteration.

**Evaluation**: It immediately reveals whether the team understands how to prioritize by business value rather than technical preference.


## 2. PI Planning Simulation (The "Big Room" Speed Run)
Objective: Practice cross-team alignment, dependency mapping, and drafting PI Objectives.

*How to Rum:*

Give each squad a blank board (or physical wall/Miro board) representing a 3-iteration Planning Interval (PI).

Give them a set of features and known cross-team dependencies (e.g., Squad A needs data from Squad B before they can build the dashboard).

Set a strict 15-minute timer for squads to negotiate timelines, resolve dependency strings (using red yarn or visual lines), and commit to a synchronized plan.

**Evaluate:** PI Planning is the heartbeat of SAFe; simulating a mini-version exposes bottlenecks in communication and highlights how well squads manage dependencies.


## 3. Flow & Bottleneck Detective (The Cumulative Flow Simulation)
Objective: Test understanding of WIP (Work in Process) limits, flow metrics, and queue policies.

**How to Rum:**

Draw or display a typical Data/Engineering value stream workflow on a board (e.g., Backlog ➔ In Development ➔ Data Contract Review ➔ Testing ➔ Done).

Introduce artificial constraints (e.g., "Testing has only 1 person, but Development has 4").

Run a quick 5-minute simulation where items move across the board, and ask the team to identify where the bottleneck occurs and how Queue Policies or swarming can fix it.

**Evaluate:** Reinforces SAFe Principle #6 (Make value flow without interruptions) and teaches teams how to manage WIP limits proactively.


## 4. The SAFe Readiness & Impediment Radar (Retrospective Style)
Objective: Evaluate team confidence across key SAFe delivery pillars (DoR, BDD, Cadence, Quality).

**How to Rum:**

Create a quick digital poll or physical radar chart with 4 axes: Clarity of Objectives, Readiness of Backlogs (DoR), Dependency Management, and Built-in Quality/Automation.

Have each team member anonymously score their confidence from 1 to 5.

Facilitate a 15-minute open discussion around the lowest-scoring axis: "What is stopping us from moving this 2 up to a 4?"

**Evaluate:** Acts as a lightweight "Measure & Grow" assessment, giving you concrete, team-generated action items for your next iteration retrospective.
