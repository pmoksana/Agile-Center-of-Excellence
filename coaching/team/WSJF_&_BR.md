## The SAFe "Backlog Refinement & WSJF" Poker (for the TEAMS)

**Objective:** Test if the team understands prioritization, economic view (SAFe Principle #1), and breaking down user stories.

**How to Run:**

Provide the team with 5–6 messy, high-level "Epic" or "Feature" descriptions related to your upcoming work.

Have them work in small groups to apply Weighted Shortest Job First (WSJF) (Cost of Delay divided by Job Size) to rank them.

Challenge them to take the top-ranked feature and slice it down into small, vertical user stories that can fit into a single 2-week iteration.

**WHY:** It immediately reveals whether the team understands how to prioritize by business value rather than technical preference.

## How to Coach the PO and the Team Through This

Coaching both sides to find the right balance:

**- Coach the PO** to use Economic Metrics (WSJF): Teach your POs to justify their priorities using WSJF (Cost of Delay divided by Job Size). If a technical item has a massive Cost of Delay (e.g., if we don't fix this broken pipeline, data ingestion stops for all clients), it rightfully moves to the top. If it's just "nice-to-have tech refactoring" with low business impact, it ranks lower. This gives the PO objective power to push back on team preferences.

**- Coach the Team** to Translate Technical Debt into Business Language: Teach engineers not to say: "We need to refactor the database schema." Instead, coach them to explain it to the PO in business terms: "If we don't refactor this schema now, every future reporting feature will take 3x longer to build, and we will miss our Q3 deliverables." When technical work is framed as a business risk, the PO can make an informed prioritization choice.

**Allocate a Fixed Capacity for Technical Enablers:**
To stop technical preference from hijacking business features, establish a healthy cadence rule—for example, dedicating 20% of every sprint's capacity exclusively to technical debt, architecture enablers, and pipeline health.
This satisfies the engineers' need for technical excellence without letting it dilute the PO's business backlog.
